# FAE Art Direction Selection Brief

State: **HUMAN_GATE — VL-06**

The machine-executable exploration is complete. The three directions are intentionally divergent and are available in the isolated preview:

https://fae-art-direction-study-3wl2o8.v2.appdeploy.ai/

## Direction A — Veil & Instrument

Dark, luminous and precise. It keeps a nocturnal FAE atmosphere but constrains it with instrument-like lines, measured data treatment and restrained signals. Mythic character comes through light, proportion and a geometric sigil rather than literal fairy imagery.

Potential strengths to judge:
- continuity with the current dark FAE world without retaining the generic dashboard grammar;
- strong technical/data compatibility;
- easiest path to expressive motion without casino language.

Potential risks to judge:
- could remain too close to familiar “dark technology” territory if refinement is timid;
- the ethereal layer must become structural rather than decorative glow.

## Direction B — Sovereign Manuscript

Light, editorial, archival and civic. It interprets sovereignty as a durable public record: folios, rules, seals, ledgers and typographic hierarchy rather than futuristic decoration.

Potential strengths to judge:
- most radical escape from generic crypto visual language;
- naturally reinforces public verification, stewardship and permanence;
- strong editorial identity for documentation and Explorer.

Potential risks to judge:
- Wallet and Mining must remain immediate rather than feeling ceremonial;
- the archival metaphor must not imply nostalgia or anti-technology.

## Direction C — Nocturne Field

Dark, spatial and sparse. It removes much of the card grammar and organizes state through fields, distance, thin signals and large compositional relationships.

Potential strengths to judge:
- most direct attack on “cards everywhere”;
- can create a highly recognizable cross-product composition system;
- provides room for a subtle mythic atmosphere without fantasy illustration.

Potential risks to judge:
- sparse composition must remain efficient for dense Explorer/data views;
- spatial elegance cannot hide transactional controls.

## What is not being selected yet

This gate does **not** freeze:
- final logo or symbol;
- exact production colors;
- exact production typefaces;
- final component tokens;
- public rebrand wording;
- any protocol/network identity.

The selection chooses the **art-direction world** to refine.

A deliberate hybrid is valid only if its synthesis is explicit (for example, B's editorial/archival typography with C's field composition). It should not become an indiscriminate mixture of all three.

After Waterfound selects the direction, Durable Execution may resume at VL-07: freeze the brand thesis + visual grammar, reconcile against newest main, then build the Homepage, Wallet and Mining vertical slices before expanding to Explorer and systemization.
