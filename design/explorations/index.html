Direction AVeil & InstrumentDark, luminous, precise. Ethereal atmosphere constrained by instrumentation. Direction BSovereign ManuscriptEditorial, archival, civic. A computational protocol presented like a durable public record. Direction CNocturne FieldSpatial, quiet, unusual. Sparse interfaces organized as fields rather than card grids.
