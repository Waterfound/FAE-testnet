## Purpose

Turn Direction D+ from a correct visual direction into an authored FAE Mining experience. The composition is intentionally judged before motion and finish.

## Composition move

The Mining surface is treated as a living computation instrument rather than a dashboard:

- one dominant work-rate object;
- a continuous computation field instead of a KPI card grid;
- accepted work as a discrete green event inside blue computational activity;
- Mining Overview as a quiet vertical operational rail;
- device telemetry as thin instrument traces, not nested cards;
- large uninterrupted white field and deliberate asymmetry;
- dark orange identity layer and dark compact network ticker;
- no gradients, glow, glass, marketing hero, casino semantics or reward-completion fiction.
