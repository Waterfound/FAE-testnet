Your Wallet stays saved in this browser/profile until you remove it or browser/device storage is cleared. FAE does not keep a server backup.

New Wallet

FAE will generate 24 recovery words locally. Store them safely offline. Anyone with the words can recover the ordinary Wallet. FAE cannot restore them for you.

Back up these 24 words

Write every word offline in the exact order. This Wallet is temporary and is not yet saved.

Verify your backup

Re-enter all 24 words. The Wallet will not be admitted to persistent storage until the checksum and exact phrase match.

Recovery Wallet

Enter the 24-word recovery phrase. Add the exact optional passphrase only when that Wallet originally used one.

Every different passphrase can derive a different valid Wallet. FAE cannot know that a passphrase is “correct” unless you recognize the derived address.
